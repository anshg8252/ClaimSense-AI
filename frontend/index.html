Welcome to ClaimSense AI

ClaimSense AI is an enterprise-grade academic decision-support platform designed to assist insurance claim assessors by cross-verifying multi-source evidence packages.

🎯

What ClaimSense AI Does

Cross-references text from insurance policies, signed claim forms, and repair invoices against computer-vision detected vehicle damage to identify documentary parity or suspicious discrepancies.

👥

Who Uses the Portal?

Claimants: Submit evidence packages, track claim progress, and download official receipts.
Assessors: Review AI damage bounding boxes, verify OCR extractions, and inspect calibrated consistency scores.

⚖️

Human-in-the-Loop Principle

The system is strictly an advisory decision-support tool. It never automatically denies or approves claims. All findings tagged as REVIEW_REQUIRED must be adjudicated by human claims adjusters.

Step-by-Step Claim Submission Tutorial

1

Step 1: Enter Claimant & Policy Details

Input your full legal name, policy number (e.g. POL-2026-9901), vehicle registration number, and the exact date of the accident.

2

Step 2: Detail the Incident Narrative

Describe how the accident occurred. Be specific about affected vehicle zones (e.g. "rear bumper dent, right tail lamp cracked"). This allows NLP algorithms to extract damaged components.

3

Step 3: Upload the 4 Mandatory Evidence Files

Upload the Policy Schedule, Claim Application Form, Repair Estimate / Invoice, and a clear Vehicle Accident Photograph.

4

Step 4: Review, Confirm & Submit

Review the populated summary cards, verify all 4 documents are checked, acknowledge the declaration checkbox, and submit for verification.

Evidence Document Ingestion Guidelines

To prevent ingestion failures and rejection by backend security validators, adhere strictly to the following standards:

Evidence Category Permitted Formats Size Limit Backend Validation Rule
Policy Schedule PDF, DOCX, PNG, JPG 16 MB Header magic byte check (%PDF-), OCR text extraction for policy validity window.
Claim Form PDF, DOCX, PNG, JPG 16 MB Tesseract OCR extraction of claimant name, vehicle number, and incident date.
Repair Estimate PDF, DOCX, PNG, JPG 16 MB Table parsing of repair line items and estimated part costs for corroboration.
Vehicle Photograph JPG, JPEG, PNG 16 MB Forensic image analysis. Synthetic solid cards or document scans are rejected with invalid_graphic. Must be a genuine vehicle photo.

Understanding Phase 7 Deterministic Verification Rules

The cross-source engine categorizes evidentiary comparisons into standard decision-support findings:

MATCH (1.00 Pts)

Documentary Parity

Identical information confirmed across two independent evidence sources (e.g., claimant name matches policy schedule via Damerau-Levenshtein distance ≥ 0.85).

MISMATCH (0.00 Pts)

Contradictory Evidence

Direct conflict detected between sources (e.g. accident date occurred before policy effective date or after policy expiration date).

INCONCLUSIVE (0.50 Pts)

Unverified / Neutral

Data was unparseable or absent. Receives neutral 50% credit rather than falsely inflating consistency or penalizing claimant.

VISUAL_ONLY (0.60 Pts)

Omitted in Invoice

Damage localized in vehicle photograph by YOLOv8, but not claimed on garage repair estimate. Flags potential pre-existing damage for adjuster review.

NOT_CONFIRMED (0.70 Pts)

Unseen Damage

Repair estimate bills for damage not visible in the provided vehicle angle. Assessor may request supplemental walkaround photos.
